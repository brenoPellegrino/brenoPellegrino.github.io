porcento-app.vercel.app

Porcento Live

Football predictions from a statistical model

Porcento turns a season of football data into clear probabilities. For each upcoming match it estimates win / draw / loss chances, a likely scoreline and projected final standings, updated round by round for regular fans rather than analysts.

I built the full product: the statistical model behind the predictions, the data that feeds it and a clean Next.js interface that makes the numbers easy to read. The interesting part is keeping projections consistent across a whole season as real results come in.

Full Stack · Statistical Modeling · Next.js · TypeScript · Supabase · Vercel

plating-five.vercel.app

Plating Live

Professional food photography from one phone photo, powered by AI

Plating helps small restaurants and bakeries get professional-looking food photography without a photo shoot. You upload a single phone photo of a dish and it generates an album of polished shots, with versions ready for different channels like menus, delivery apps and social.

I built the whole thing: the generative-AI pipeline, the backend and API integration that runs it, user accounts with an album and credits system and the Next.js interface. Then I deployed it. The real challenge is turning an amateur photo into consistent, on-brand results a business can actually publish.

AI · Full Stack · Next.js · TypeScript · Generative AI · Vercel

About

From running a business to shipping software

I’m a software engineer based in Florida. I came to engineering from a slightly unusual path: a background in engineering and several years owning and running a hospitality business before moving into software full time.

That path shapes how I work. Running a business meant owning outcomes end to end: operations, finances and customers, with problems that had to be solved under pressure and with real money on the line. I bring that same ownership to engineering. I think about the user and the business behind a feature, communicate clearly with the people around me and follow work all the way from idea to production.

Today I focus on backend and full-stack development: C#/.NET services and APIs, React/TypeScript front ends, relational databases, event-driven messaging and building LLM and agent features that hold up in production.
